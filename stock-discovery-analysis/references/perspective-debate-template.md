# Perspective Debate Template

Use in **standard** and **deep** modes after core business and valuation work. This replaces generic bull/bear labels with **methodology-based perspectives** that can genuinely conflict.

Do not use this before valuation scenarios are complete.

## Four Perspectives

| Perspective | Core question | Typical strength | Typical blind spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business Essence** | Is this a good business with durable advantage? | Moat, unit economics, value capture | Ignores price and timing |
| **Financial Quality** | Is it earning real money at a safe valuation? | Cash flow, balance sheet, margin of safety | Misses optionality or turnaround |
| **Inversion & Competition** | How could we be wrong? Why wouldn't smart money buy? | Anti-consensus, failure paths, crowded trade detection | Can become overly pessimistic |
| **Long-term Risk** | Will this still matter in 5–10 years? | Structural trend, governance, disruption risk | Underweights near-term catalysts |

## Hard Rules

- Sentiment cannot override Business Essence or Financial Quality alone.
- A strong industry narrative cannot override Inversion & Competition concerns without evidence.
- If perspectives materially conflict, final classification cannot be strong without naming the tradeoff.
- In quick mode, skip this template unless user explicitly asks for debate.
